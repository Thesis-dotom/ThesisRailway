Scoring Framework

Quality and relative valuation are scored independently. Every usable company stays accessible. The strict research screen requires a combined score of 150+ and Quality of at least 60; a separate quality watchlist starts at 70. These provisional thresholds have not been backtested.

Business Quality Rating

  • Operating return on capital — 20
  • Three-year operating margin — 15
  • Three-year free-cash-flow margin — 15
  • Cash-flow consistency — 10
  • Net debt / EBITDA — 15
  • Annualized revenue growth — 10
  • Annualized diluted EPS growth — 15

Opportunity Rating

  • Annual earnings yield vs. peers — 35
  • Annual free-cash-flow yield vs. peers — 35
  • Annual EBITDA / enterprise value vs. peers — 30
  • Analyst targets and momentum are context only.

About This Prototype

Thesis is designed to make company research clearer and more structured. Ratings are analytical estimates, not guarantees or personalized recommendations.

This is an educational prototype. It uses public market data through a non-official wrapper and should not be treated as financial advice.
