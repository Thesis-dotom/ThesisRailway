Disclosures & privacy

Use Thesis as a starting point for your own company research.

Research and investment risk

Thesis provides educational company research, not personalized investment, legal or tax advice. Scores, screen matches and company-finder results are not buy or sell instructions. The finder filters the research universe using your selected preferences; it does not assess your finances, risk capacity or suitability.

All investments can lose value. High scores do not guarantee returns, and low scores can overlook worthwhile companies. The model has not been performance-backtested. Verify the business, valuation and risks independently.

Data and accuracy

Market and financial data come from Yahoo Finance through yfinance. Data can be delayed, incomplete, revised or inaccurate. Check material figures against company filings and investor-relations materials. Source links do not imply automatic editorial review. The limited research sample does not cover the entire market.

Company suggestions

Suggestions contain a company name, optional ticker and note, submission time, reference and review status. They are stored privately for the site owner to review. No email address or personal financial information is requested; do not include either in the free-text field.

Submitting does not guarantee coverage, a reply or a favorable score. The owner can mark suggestions as reviewed, shortlisted or dismissed. The system does not send email, publish submissions or automatically add companies to the ranked universe.

Submitted text remains in the owner's database until the owner removes it. Application rate-limit records use keyed hashes of a network address, rather than storing the raw address in the suggestion tables, and are cleared after expiry during later requests. Hosting providers may retain access logs.

Cookies and research preferences

The suggestion and owner pages use a signed session cookie for form protection and owner sign-in. Owner sessions expire after one hour. Research preferences appear in the page URL and are not stored as a personal profile. This build includes no advertising or analytics trackers.
